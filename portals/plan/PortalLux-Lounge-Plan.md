## Description

About page
You're great at making things. Running the business behind them is a different job: orders in your DMs, supplies you forgot to reorder, follow-ups you never sent, and the same tasks every day by hand.
PortalLux Lounge is where you build the back office of your business: one branded portal that shows your orders, inventory, customers and finances at a glance, and a schedule that handles your daily and weekly tasks for you. ## Tiers and classes

Each tier includes everything below it. Classes open one at a time, in order.

### Lobby (free) (6 classes)

#### L1 · Welcome to the Lounge

- Outcome: Members know how the community works, pick their track and post an introduction.
- Lessons:
  - Welcome to the building (5 min): Walk through the Lobby, Suite and Penthouse tiers using your Tower cover. Show where classes, live builds and wins live.
  - What you will build (6 min): Screen-share your real portals: Today page, Orders, Filament library, Reports. Blur customer names.
  - Pick your track (4 min): Print and Laser (Allure Fusions), Handmade and Beauty (Madam Allure), Any Small Shop. Show one page from each.
  - How to get help and post wins (3 min): The Ask for Help and Wins categories, and the weekly live build time.

#### L2 · The Blueprint

- Outcome: Members list every job they do and see which ones a portal and automation can take over.
- Lessons:
  - The hats you wear (6 min): Every job in a small business: making, selling, shipping, messages, money, marketing.
  - Your job inventory (8 min): Fill in the job inventory live. Show your own: Madam Allure, Allure Fusions and AI AuthoriTech.
  - Automate, Assist or Keep (7 min): Sort each job. Show your automations board (Running, Ready to Build, Planned).

#### L3 · The Essentials

- Outcome: Members gather everything their portal will need: products, prices, policies, suppliers and where orders come from, in one place.
- Lessons:
  - What your portal needs to know (6 min): Show the information behind your own portals: catalog, price list, policies, suppliers.
  - Products, prices and policies (8 min): Collect every product or service with sizes and prices, plus deposit, shipping and return rules.
  - Customers, suppliers and order sources (6 min): Where orders come in today, who you buy from, and where your customer list lives. Never share real customer details in AI chats.

#### L4 · Connections Check

- Outcome: Members list the tools they already use, decide what needs to connect to their portal, and set up a free AI assistant safely.
- Lessons:
  - Your tools today (6 min): Sales, payments, invoices, email, social, storage. Show the tools behind your businesses: Wave, Clover, GoHighLevel, OneDrive.
  - What needs to sync (8 min): Which information should flow into the portal, and how often. Exports now, automatic syncing later in the Suite.
  - Your AI assistant, set up safely (8 min): Pick a free AI tool and learn the ground rules: no passwords or keys, example data only, you approve everything.

#### L5 · Signature Style

- Outcome: Members gather their brand kit: colors, fonts, logo files and a brand card they will paste into every build later.
- Lessons:
  - Your colors and fonts (6 min): Pick three colors and two fonts. Show how each of your businesses has its own accent.
  - Your logo files (6 min): What files you need (a transparent logo, a square icon) and where to keep them.
  - Your brand card (8 min): Use the brand card builder in the Class Studio. It is ready for the first build in the Suite.

#### L6 · The Portal Plan

- Outcome: Members turn their blueprint, essentials, connections and brand kit into one portal plan: the pages they need and what their Today page should show.
- Lessons:
  - The pages your portal needs (6 min): Pick main pages from your blueprint. Show the left menu and sub-pages of your own portals.
  - What Today should show you (6 min): Due, late, low, unanswered: choose your top five.
  - Your finished plan and what comes next (6 min): Everything is ready to build. Show what the Suite builds with this plan, one week at a time, starting with the first portal.

### Suite (Builder) (13 classes)

#### 1 · The First Portal

- Outcome: Members make a working one-page HTML portal with free AI and open it in their browser.
- Lessons:
  - What a portal is (4 min): A private web page on your computer that shows your business at a glance. No hosting, no code skills.
  - Build it with one prompt (12 min): Paste the starter prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Save the reply as portal.html and open it.
  - Make it yours with your brand card (8 min): Paste the brand card from Signature Style so it is in their colors from the start.
  - Save it safely (5 min): Put it in a OneDrive or Google Drive folder and pin a shortcut. Never paste customer names into a shared chat.

#### 2 · The Grand Lobby

- Outcome: Members build a real portal shell in their brand and save a brand card they reuse in every build.
- Lessons:
  - Your brand card (8 min): Colors, fonts, logo, light and dark mode, glow trim. Show your own: rose gold, sapphire, teal.
  - The portal shell (12 min): Left menu of main pages, sub-pages across the top, even cards. Use the Prompt Book "Portal shell" prompt.
  - One look, many businesses (8 min): Show how each of your portals uses its own accent color with one shared design.
  - Automate It: brand card in every prompt (6 min): Paste the brand card at the top of every build so every page matches.

#### 3 · The Skills Vault

- Outcome: Members turn what they have already done in AI chats into reusable skill cards.
- Lessons:
  - Find your best past chats (8 min): Search your ChatGPT history for work you redo.
  - Write a skill card (10 min): Goal, inputs, steps, output, rules.
  - Automate It: one library for every AI (7 min): Keep cards in one Google Doc and paste them into any tool.

#### 4 · The Daily Rhythm

- Outcome: Every daily, weekly and monthly task is on a calendar and marked Automate, Assist or Keep.
- Lessons:
  - Daily, weekly, monthly (8 min): Turn the job inventory into a rhythm.
  - Put it on a calendar (8 min): Time blocks for making, selling and admin.
  - Automate It: your first scheduled task (10 min): Show your 6:30 AM daily refresh and Monday snapshot.

#### 5 · The Front Desk

- Outcome: A custom order form with lead times that lands new orders on the board.
- Lessons:
  - What every order needs (8 min): Fields, deposits and due dates.
  - Build the form (12 min): Show your New order page with deposit math.
  - Automate It: confirmation message (8 min): Auto-filled first name, branded email.

#### 6 · The Order Runway

- Outcome: Every order moves from Inquiry to Shipped on one board.
- Lessons:
  - Stages every order moves through (8 min): Inquiry to Shipped.
  - Build the pipeline (15 min): Show your pipeline and shipping log.
  - Automate It: 7 AM late-order check (10 min): Flag at-risk orders every morning.

#### 7 · Plugged In

- Outcome: Shop exports and sheets feed one data file that refreshes daily.
- Lessons:
  - Where your data lives (8 min): Wave, Clover, GoHighLevel, Etsy, Shopify.
  - Import with CSV (12 min): Show the customer import.
  - Automate It: daily refresh and synced folder (12 min): Save data to OneDrive and refresh on a schedule.

#### 8 · The Stockroom

- Outcome: A stock list for filament, ingredients or supplies with low-stock alerts.
- Lessons:
  - What to track (8 min): Materials, packaging, equipment.
  - Build the inventory (15 min): Show the filament library with QR spool stickers and ingredients list.
  - Automate It: reorder alert (8 min): Low stock shows on Today.

#### 9 · The Lounge View

- Outcome: Members build their Today page and morning dashboard from their Portal Plan and install it as an app on phone and desktop.
- Lessons:
  - Your Today page (10 min): Build the Today cards chosen in the Portal Plan: due, late, low, unanswered.
  - The full dashboard (12 min): Show your Morning Dashboard across businesses.
  - Automate It: install as an app (8 min): Install from Chrome or Edge, Add to Home Screen.

#### 10 · The Concierge Desk

- Outcome: A message library in their voice, branded email, and a follow-up survey.
- Lessons:
  - Your voice in templates (10 min): Rewrite templates with personality.
  - Branded email and signature (10 min): Logo, closing, name, business, tagline, phone.
  - Automate It: survey 12 days after delivery (8 min): 6 questions, wish list, referrals.

#### 11 · After Hours

- Outcome: The full daily and weekly schedule runs on its own after hours, tracked on an automations board.
- Lessons:
  - Your automations board (8 min): Running, Ready to Build, Planned.
  - Chain the schedule (15 min): Daily refresh, weekly snapshot, reports.
  - Automate It: AI assistant on social (10 min): Muse working your Instagram and Facebook goals.

#### 12 · The Rate Card

- Outcome: Members build a rate card: costs, pricing that covers their time, and a weekly business summary with exports.
- Lessons:
  - Know your costs (10 min): Materials, time, packaging.
  - Price with confidence (10 min): Show the formula builder costs and time pricing at $125 an hour.
  - Automate It: Monday one-pager (10 min): Reports with Excel, PDF and Word exports.

#### 13 · The Back Office

- Outcome: Members set up their back office: SOPs, a settings or formula library, a helper handoff and a maintenance routine.
- Lessons:
  - Write your SOPs (10 min): Step-by-step for repeat work.
  - Hand it off (10 min): What a helper or VA needs.
  - Automate It: health check and skills refresh (8 min): Weekly and monthly upkeep.

### Penthouse (premium) (6 classes)

#### B1 · The Floor Plan

- Outcome: Members plan an interactive website as a building: lobby, a suite for each business, lounges.
- Lessons:
  - From pages to places (10 min): Show the Crawford Creative Suites entrance, lobby and suites.
  - Map your building (12 min): Floors, desks, lounges for each brand.

#### B2 · Set the Scene

- Outcome: Consistent lobby, desk and lounge images made in ChatGPT in brand colors.
- Lessons:
  - Style lock prompts (12 min): Keep every scene matching.
  - Desk and lounge scenes (12 min): Names on desks, check-in tablets.

#### B3 · The Elevator

- Outcome: Elevator floors, check-in tablets and product buttons that open shop pages.
- Lessons:
  - Elevator and floors (12 min): Show the elevator choosing a floor.
  - Check-in and product buttons (12 min): Tablet opens options; buttons jump to products.

#### B4 · The Concierge Voice

- Outcome: A concierge and receptionists with natural voices and lobby music.
- Lessons:
  - Choosing voices (10 min): Speed, warmth, license limits.
  - Recording the lines (12 min): Greeting, replies, elevator lines; music from Suno.

#### B5 · Grand Opening

- Outcome: Free hosting with a preview-and-approve routine and hide-from-search until ready.
- Lessons:
  - Free hosting (12 min): GitHub Pages.
  - Preview, approve, publish (10 min): Your publishing workflow; rollback tags.

#### B6 · The Private Entrance

- Outcome: Website orders and forms flow into the portal built in the Suite.
- Lessons:
  - Forms to portal (12 min): Project forms feed Orders.
  - Keep it private (8 min): Public site shows counts, portal holds names.
